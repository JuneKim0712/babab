PriceOracle settings

Your context.dev key is stored locally in chrome.storage.local and only ever sent to context.dev from the extension's background service worker. Gemini prediction-market data is public and needs no key.

Where PriceOracle appears

Changes apply immediately, including on Amazon pages that are already open.

Price alerts

Alerts are saved in this browser, not on a server, and there is nothing to sign in to. When an item reaches your target PriceOracle shows a Chrome notification and puts a count on its toolbar icon. They also appear on the PriceOracle alerts page next to the alerts you already have there. Set an alert from the card on any product page.

Kept on this device. It is listed on your alerts, and the notification gets an "Email me this" button that opens a message to this address in your mail app. PriceOracle does not send email itself.

context.dev API key

Used to extract pricing signals from the Amazon product page. A working default key is pre-filled; replace it with your own any time. Responses are cached for 24 hours per ASIN to save credits.

Local data

PriceOracle remembers the prices it has seen for each product (up to 300 products, 120 days) and uses them to sharpen the projection once there is enough. This stays in your browser and is never sent anywhere.
