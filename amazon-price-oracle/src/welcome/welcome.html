No setup needed. Open any Amazon product page and a Buy Timing card appears right under the price.

Try it on a sample product →
  1. Browse Amazon as usual. Already-open Amazon tabs work immediately, no reload.
  2. Read the card. WAIT, BUY NOW or FAIR PRICE, plus five projected 30-day price tiers.
  3. Pin the icon (puzzle piece in the toolbar, then the pin) for quick stats.

Live Fed and recession odds come from Gemini prediction markets, with no key required. API keys are optional and live in Settings.
